Help & tips

Quick how-tos for the trays app and the Biologics app. Tap a section to open it.

Getting aroundSwitching apps, settings, adding to your home screen
  • Switch apps: tap the app name at the top left to jump between Inventory Requests (trays) and Biologics.
  • Settings: tap your name or the gear at the top right for Account (your role, sign out) and, for admins, Team.
  • On a phone, the tabs sit along the bottom. Anything that doesn't fit is under More.
  • Add it to your home screen: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen; in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It then opens like an app.
  • If you lose signal, a bar says you're offline. Changes save once you're back online.
Requesting trays Sales repsNew requests, adding trays, editing and cancelling
  1. Open Add Request. Fill in the surgery date, account, territory and surgeon.
  2. If the surgeon has a preference card, pick the procedure and their usual trays fill in for you. Add or remove trays as needed.
  3. Only set Date required if you need the trays earlier than the day before surgery.
  4. Tap Submit. Everything on one submit is one case.

Forgot a tray? In My requests → Upcoming, open the case and tap + Add trays to this case.

Edit or cancel: open the case in My requests and use Edit or Cancel. Cancel asks you to tap a second time to confirm. If a tray was already pulled, the inventory team gets a heads-up to put it back.

Status on each case: Requested → Being pulled → All pulled → Delivered.

Pulling and sending trays Inventory teamThe pull queue, heads-up alerts and colors

Requests is the pull queue, sorted by pull-by date. Trays are pulled 1 business day before they're sent, and sent the day before surgery unless the rep set a date required.

  • Open a case and tick a tray when it's pulled. Untick it to undo.
  • Yellow means the case is in progress (something pulled or sent). Green means every tray has been delivered.

The Heads up box lists things to act on. Tap the button once each one is handled:

  • Loaner needed means every set is booked for those days. Order a loaner, then tap Loaner ordered.
  • Urgent return means a case is waiting on a set coming straight back from another case. Tap Arranged once that's sorted.
  • Late request, Changed after pull and Put back (cancelled after it was pulled) are there so nothing slips by.
Schedule, delivery and returnsDelivery photos, return photos, missing items

Schedule shows every case by surgery date (reps see their own territory first). Tap a case to add delivery photos and mark trays Delivered.

Returns lists cases once surgery day arrives; trays are due back 1 business day after.

  1. Tap the case, then Return a tray.
  2. Take a photo and say whether anything is missing. The delivery photos show above so you can compare.
  3. If nothing's missing, the case leaves the page and the return photos are deleted to save space. If something's missing, it stays, and the photos are kept until it's found.

Photos are deleted automatically after 2 weeks (except trays still missing items).

Keep patient labels and patient information out of photos.

Inventory, analysis and SST Inventory teamSet counts, low inventory, loaner trends
  • Inventory counts are the total sets you own, on the shelf or out on a case. Pulling and returning don't change them. What's free each day is worked out from the requests, and earlier surgeries get sets first.
  • Mark a tray Low inventory when you can't count on a set coming back in time. It then goes straight to Loaner needed instead of asking for an urgent return.
  • Analysis ranks the trays you keep running short on. The ones at the top are worth stocking more of.
  • SST lists SST cases by the day they need to be sent, with a send-out report.
Starred trays Product managersFollow the trays you care about

Tap the ☆ next to any tray, anywhere in the app. Every case using one of your starred trays then shows on My Trays, by surgery date.

Preference cardsSave a surgeon's usual trays

Under Pref Cards (on a phone: More → Preference Cards), save each surgeon's usual trays for a procedure. On a new request, picking that surgeon and procedure fills the trays in for you.

Biologics appRequesting, packing, used/returned and POs

Open it from the app name at the top left.

  • Request: type the ABS number or part of the description and pick the graft. Add as many grafts as the case needs, then submit. To add more later, open the case and add to it.
  • Pack (inventory team): enter the expiration for each graft as you pack it.
  • After surgery (anyone): mark grafts Used or Returned. For returns, answer Returned to shelf/freezer?. If not yet, it's held until someone puts it away. Returns can include a photo.
  • PO page: used grafts show up there. The rep adds the PO number, and an admin closes it.
  • Link: the inventory team can paste a link on a case (one per case). It stays with the case from request to PO, and anyone can tap it to open.
Requests from the Google SheetsWhy some requests say “Google Sheet”

Through October, requests entered in the INVENTORY REQUESTS and BIOLOGICS Google Sheets are copied into the app automatically about once an hour. Only requests for today or later are copied, and each one only once. After a request is copied, its status is tracked in the app, so make any changes here rather than in the sheet.
